Data & disclaimer

Built for exploration, not evacuation decisions.

FloodGuard India combines Open-Meteo's GloFAS river-discharge forecasts and ERA5-Land historical weather with a locally trained deep-learning model. It is an experimental decision-support demo. Always follow IMD, CWC and local administration warnings.
